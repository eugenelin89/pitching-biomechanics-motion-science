# Pitching Biomechanics & Motion Science (PBMS)

A **24-month independent study program** for learning the anatomy, physics, quantitative movement analysis, research methods, and scientific measurement principles relevant to baseball pitching.

## Start here

**[Read the complete PBMS curriculum →](PROGRAM.md)**

The program includes:
- A course crosswalk based on UBC Kinesiology and Stanford Biomechanical Engineering.
- Twelve self-study modules across four six-month semesters.
- Recommended textbooks, open university materials, research papers, software and datasets.
- A first-30-days checklist and month-by-month learning roadmap.
- Assignments, biomechanics labs, reliability/validation exercises, and a reproducible capstone.
- Assessment criteria, ethics, athlete privacy, and dataset-licensing considerations.

**Recommended pace:** 8–12 hours/week (approximately 830–1,250 hours across 24 months).

**First action:** Open [Section 7: Your First 30 Days](PROGRAM.md#7-your-first-30-days) and begin Week 1.

> PBMS is an independently authored educational curriculum, not an accredited university program or a medical, athletic-training, or coaching credential. It is informed by publicly available university course information but is not affiliated with or endorsed by UBC or Stanford.
